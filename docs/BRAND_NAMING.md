# The platform name — brief, candidates, and what must be checked before choosing

Drafted 2026-09-21. Ten candidates, the reasoning behind each, and the three checks that decide
whether any of them can actually be used.

---

## 1. The brief, read back

Before proposing anything, here is what I understood. **If any line is wrong, the names below are
built on it and should be thrown away.**

### What the name is for

| | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | The **platform / company** brand. One name for everything we do: the LINE OA, the DevOps console, invoices, contracts, the Play Store developer name, the agreements |
| **Not** | The name a school's parents see. Each school renames the app to their own school |
| **So it is an *ingredient* brand** | Like "Intel Inside" or "Powered by Shopify" — it appears in one small line under somebody else's name: **ขับเคลื่อนโดย ___** · **Powered by ___** |

That last point is the one that shapes everything. A name that must survive being **small, secondary,
and next to a school's own logo** has to be short, confident and easy to say once. Long names die in
that slot.

### Hard constraints

- **5–7 letters**
- **Must contain A and T**
- A coined word — **no existing dictionary meaning**
- Every letter carries its own meaning
- Reads modern, sounds good said aloud, feels new
- Feeling in the family of: *Absolute, Abyss, Astonishing, Authentic, Ambitious, Autonomous*

### Why A

Three reasons, and they stack:

1. **The school that gave us the start** — Atom Nursery. The A is theirs before it is ours.
2. **The first letter of the alphabet** — where all learning begins, which is literally the business.
3. **It is in every name in your family.**

That is a real origin story, not a marketing one. It should be told, not hidden.

### Two constraints I have added, from the market

These were not in the brief but the name fails without them:

- **It must be easy for a Thai speaker to say on the first try.** The people saying it out loud are
  Thai directors, teachers and parents. So: no consonant clusters Thai does not use, nothing that
  needs explaining twice on the phone.
- **It must not collide with a Thai word that carries the wrong feeling.** One example that ruled a
  whole shape out: anything starting **ATTA-** is out, because **อัตตา** means *ego / self-regard* in
  Thai and Pali. "Attara" sounds fine to an English ear and reads as self-importance to a Thai one.

## 2. The ten

Each letter maps to something the product actually does. Thai pronunciation is written the way a
Thai speaker would read it cold.

### ① ATOVIA · อะ-โท-เวีย · 6

> **A**ccuracy · **T**rust · **O**pen · **V**oice · **I**nsight · **A**scend

**Story — the strongest of the ten.** *Via* is the road. **ATOVIA is "the road from Atom"** — named
for the nursery that gave the project its first chance, pointing at everywhere it goes next. The
origin is built into the name without the name belonging to one customer.

Sounds modern, three clean syllables, no Thai collision. Carries the A twice — opening and closing.

---

### ② ATHERA · อะ-เท-รา · 6

> **A**ccuracy · **T**rust · **H**ome · **E**very child · **R**ecord · **A**lways

**Story.** *Ether* — the medium everything else moves through, invisible and everywhere. The school's
name is on the app; **Athera is the air it runs in.** Exactly the ingredient-brand position.

---

### ③ AVENTA · อะ-เวน-ตา · 6

> **A**ccuracy · **V**isible · **E**very child · **N**urture · **T**rust · **A**lways

**Story.** From *advent* — an arrival, a beginning. The word for the moment something starts. Firm,
short, easy to say, sits well in a small line under a school's logo.

---

### ④ ATRINA · อะ-ทริ-นา · 6

> **A**ttendance · **T**rust · **R**ecord · **I**nsight · **N**urture · **A**ccuracy

**Story.** The six things the platform does, in order of a school day: who is here, what was
promised, what was written down, what it tells you, the care around it, and the money being exactly
right. Softer and warmer than the others — the most "nursery" of the ten.

---

### ⑤ ARATA · อา-รา-ตา · 5 — *the shortest*

> **A**ccuracy · **R**ecord · **A**ttendance · **T**rust · **A**scend

**Story.** *Arata* (新) is Japanese for **new, fresh, begun again**. Five letters, three syllables,
three A's. The shortest name here and the easiest to say — which matters most in the one place it
will actually appear.

⚠️ It is a real Japanese word and a Japanese given name. That cuts both ways: a meaning to tell, and
a claim we do not fully own.

---

### ⑥ AVANTRA · อะ-วาน-ตรา · 7 — *the longest*

> **A**ccuracy · **V**isible · **A**ccountable · **N**urture · **T**rust · **R**ecord · **A**lways

**Story.** *Avant* — ahead, in front. The name of a platform that intends to be where schools are
going rather than where they are. The most ambitious-sounding of the ten, and the one that most
needs the small-line test before you commit: seven letters is a lot under somebody else's logo.

---

### ⑦ ALTEVA · อัล-เท-วา · 6

> **A**ccuracy · **L**edger · **T**rust · **E**very child · **V**isible · **A**lways

**Story.** *Alt* — high, raised, the level above. **L for Ledger is deliberate**: this is a platform
whose first duty is that the money is exactly right, and it says so in its own name. The most
"serious institution" of the ten.

---

### ⑧ ATRIVA · อะ-ทรี-วา · 6

> **A**ttendance · **T**rust · **R**ecord · **I**nsight · **V**isible · **A**ccuracy

**Story.** Fully coined — it means nothing in any language, which is the cleanest possible position
to own. Modern, technical, confident. The name that sounds most like software and least like a
school.

---

### ⑨ AURENTA · อู-เรน-ตา · 7

> **A**ccuracy · **U**nified · **R**ecord · **E**very child · **N**urture · **T**rust · **A**lways

**Story.** *Aurum* — gold. Not wealth: the standard. A gold standard for how a school's records and
money are kept. Warm sound, rounder than the rest.

---

### ⑩ ASTERA · อัส-เท-รา · 6

> **A**ccuracy · **S**afety · **T**rust · **E**very child · **R**ecord · **A**scend

**Story.** *Aster* — star. The thing you navigate by, and the thing every parent calls their child.
The most emotionally direct name here.

⚠️ Highest collision risk of the ten — "Astera" is an established stage-lighting brand and the shape
is popular. Check hardest before falling in love with it.

## 3. Which three I would shortlist, and why

| | |
|---|---|
| 🥇 **ATOVIA** | The only one whose **story cannot be copied**, because it is true. "The road from Atom" gives the school that took the first risk a permanent place in the brand, tells well in one sentence to a director, and still belongs to no single customer. Six letters, three syllables, no Thai collision |
| 🥈 **ARATA** | Because **five letters is a genuine advantage** in the one slot the name has to live in, and "new beginning" is the business. The Japanese meaning is a bonus and a caveat at once |
| 🥉 **ALTEVA** | Because **L for Ledger is the most honest letter in this document.** A platform for schools whose credibility rests on money being exact, saying so inside its own name |

## 4. Before any of this is chosen — three checks I cannot do

⚠️ **I cannot verify availability. Nothing above should be treated as free to use until these pass.**

| | Check | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Trademark**, class 42 (software) and class 41 (education) | กรมทรัพย์สินทางปัญญา (DBD/DIP) search, and a quick global look |
| **2** | **Domains** — `.com` first, `.co.th` if we register a Thai company | Any registrar |
| **3** | **Handles** — LINE OA id, Facebook, Instagram, and the **Play Store developer name** | Each platform |

**Run all three on your top three before deciding.** A name that fails check 1 after the LINE OA,
the contracts and the Play Store listing all carry it is a very expensive kind of late.

### One more test, and it is the real one

Say the shortlist out loud, in Thai, in the sentence it will actually live in:

> "ระบบของโรงเรียนเราใช้ **___** ครับ"
> "ขับเคลื่อนโดย **___**"

Then have somebody who has never seen it written **spell it back after hearing it once.** A name that
survives that is a name that survives a phone call with a director — which is how the first eight
schools are going to arrive.
